Cloud proposal software is critical for commercial AV project management because large-scale projects — stadiums, corporate campuses, restaurants and bars, houses of worship — involve more line items, more trades, more stakeholders, and more money at risk. A cloud platform that connects the proposal directly to scheduling, procurement, design documentation, team handoffs, and progress billing closes the gap between what was quoted and what gets built, protecting both the deadline and the margin.
