The data is unambiguous: proposal speed and accuracy directly determine whether a residential AV, home theater, smart home, or security integrator wins the job. According to D-Tools' 2025 Year-in-Review Report , integrators who deliver a proposal to a client within 48 hours days close 88% of the time, compared with just 69.6% for integrators whose proposals take 60 days or more to reach the client. For an industry where the average gross margins on equipment are getting tighter, an 18-point swing in win rate is a big difference between having a full pipeline versus an empty one.
